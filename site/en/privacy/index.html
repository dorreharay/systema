Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what data the re:string app (the “app”) and the website tryrestring.com collect, why, where it is stored, for how long, and how you can delete it. We wrote it in plain language.

In short

  • re:string is an app for racket stringers. What you enter (clients, rackets, strings, services, orders) is used only to make the app work for you.
  • We show no ads, do not track you, use no third-party analytics or crash reporting, and do not sell data.
  • Your data is stored on a server in the EU: Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it in the app at any time: Settings → Account → Delete account.

1. Who we are

The app and the website are provided by Velar (the “operator”, “we”). For any question about privacy, write to support@tryrestring.com.

For your account data (email, password or Google or Apple identifier, settings) we are the data controller. You enter the data about your own clients yourself: you decide what to keep, and we process it only to make the app work for you and never use it for our own purposes. If you keep records of clients, make sure that this complies with the law of your country.

2. What data we process

Account

  • Email — required: for signing in and for the password reset email. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email from them.
  • Password. It is not stored in plain text anywhere: only a bcrypt hash is kept, from which the password cannot be recovered.
  • Name — optional.
  • Workshop settings: name, currency, tension unit (kg or lbs), time zone, language, whether reminders are on, the order number counter.

Signing in with Google or Apple

  • Google. The app gets a token from Google and the server checks it. We store only the identifier of your Google account and the email and name that Google gives. We do not store Google’s tokens and never see your Google password.
  • Apple. The server checks Apple’s token. We store the Apple user identifier, the email that Apple passes on (the real one or, if you hide it, a private relay address ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com) and, for a new account, the name (Apple gives it only the first time you sign in). If Apple issues the app a refresh token, we store that too: it is used only to revoke the sign-in at Apple when you delete the account.

An account created with Google or Apple has no password. If you first signed up with a password and later sign in with Google or Apple using the same verified email, it is the same account: its password is removed and open sign-ins end. To sign in with a password again, reset it (“Forgot password?”).

What you enter in the app

  • Clients: name, phone, email (optional), notes.
  • Rackets: brand, model, notes, stock count.
  • Strings: brand, model, gauge, type, price, stock count.
  • Services: name, price, kind.
  • Orders: number, status, received and due dates, payment, chosen strings, tension, knots, notes, prices and total.
  • Photos of rackets attached to orders (JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, up to 10 per order). At the moment the app does not offer adding new photos; photos added earlier can be viewed and deleted.

A new account starts with a catalog of common rackets and strings (none in stock) and a few typical services without prices. Each account sees only its own records.

Signing in

After you sign in, the server issues a signed session token that is valid for 7 days. The app keeps it in the iPhone’s secure storage (Keychain); the server keeps no list of sessions. The server also sends this token as an HttpOnly cookie, which is used for signing in only. The interface language chosen before sign-in is stored on the phone in the same way.

Password reset

When you ask for a link (“Forgot password?”), the server creates a one-time random token. Only its SHA-256 hash and the expiry time (60 minutes) are stored in the database. We email you the link. This is the only email the app sends: we send no marketing.

Technical data

  • Server logs: time, request address and the text of a technical error. If an email could not be sent, the log keeps the recipient’s address and the subject.
  • IP address. To stop password guessing, the server counts sign-in attempts per IP address — in memory only, for 15 minutes; it is not written to the database. The hosting provider, as network infrastructure, sees the IP addresses of requests and may keep its own connection logs.

Device permissions

  • Notifications. If you turn reminders on, the app asks iOS for permission and shows reminders about orders that are due (on the morning of the due day, or an hour before the time you set). They are scheduled on the phone itself: no server and no push notification service is involved, and we do not receive a device token. The text of a reminder (order number, client’s name, racket) is shown on your phone’s screen.
  • Camera and photo library. The app can access them only when you yourself want to take or choose a photo of a racket, and only with your permission in iOS; we cannot see the rest of your photos. You can withdraw the permission in iOS settings. At the moment adding photos is switched off in the app.

The buttons “Call”, “Send a text message” and “Email” open the Phone, Messages or Mail app with the client’s number or address: you send the message yourself. re:string does not write to your clients.

Contacting support

If you write to support@tryrestring.com, we receive your address and the text of your message and use them only to reply to you.

What we do not collect

The app has no analytics, advertising or automatic crash-reporting libraries. We do not collect your location, do not read your phone’s contacts, do not use the advertising identifier and do not track you across apps and websites.

3. Why we process it

  • To create your account (with a password or through Google or Apple), give you access to the app and show your records and calculations (month overview, debts, stock) — performance of our agreement with you (Terms of Service).
  • To send the password reset email when you ask for it — performance of our agreement with you.
  • To protect the service: limiting sign-in attempts, checking tokens, investigating technical failures — our legitimate interest in security.
  • To answer your request to support — legitimate interest and performance of our agreement.
  • Reminders — your consent (the iOS permission), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To comply with a legal obligation, if there is one.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, we make no automated decisions about you and we do not sell data. We have technical access to the database, but we do not read your records without need: only to support you at your request, for security or when the law requires it.

4. Who can access it

We use a few services. They process data only so that the app and the website can work:

Railway (USA)
Hosting of the app’s server, of the PostgreSQL database and of the website tryrestring.com in the EU West region (Amsterdam, the Netherlands). All account data is stored here.
Resend (USA)
Sends the password reset email: receives your address, your name (if given) and the text of the email with the link.
Expo (USA)
The update service. When the app starts, it asks this service whether a newer version of its code is available and downloads it. The request contains technical details (platform, app version, update channel), your IP address and may contain an anonymous installation identifier; it contains no account data and none of your records. Expo’s policy: expo.dev/privacy.
Namecheap (USA)
Domain registrar, DNS and forwarding of mail from the support address to our mailbox.
Google (USA)
Handles signing in with Google, if you choose it. Signing in happens between your device and Google; our server only checks the token that Google issued to the app and sees in it the account identifier, the email and the name. Google’s policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
Apple (USA)
Distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight. Also handles signing in with Apple, if you choose it: our server checks Apple’s token, receives the user identifier, the email (the real one, or a private relay address if you hide it) and the name, and when you delete the account asks Apple to revoke the sign-in. Emails that we send to a relay address are forwarded by Apple to your real email. If you allowed iOS to share diagnostics with developers, Apple may pass us anonymised crash reports; they do not contain your records.

We do not pass data to advertising networks or data brokers. We may disclose data if the law or a court requires it.

Some of these companies are based in the United States, so technical data may be processed there. For such transfers they rely on the EU standard contractual clauses and/or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything you created in it are kept until you delete the account.
  • The Google or Apple identifier, the Apple refresh token, and the email and name we received from them are kept in the account and deleted with it.
  • Orders. “Delete Order” erases it for good together with its photos and services; what the order took from the stock is returned.
  • Photos. A photo you remove is erased when you save the order.
  • Clients, rackets, strings. “Delete” removes the record from your lists, but the record itself stays in the database so that past orders keep the names. It is erased for good together with the account. If you need a record erased earlier, write to us. Services are archived, not deleted, and are also erased with the account.
  • A password reset token is valid for 60 minutes and only once.
  • Correspondence with support is kept as long as it is needed to help you.
  • Server logs are kept by the hosting provider under its own rules, for a limited time.

6. How to delete your account and data

  1. Open the “Settings” tab.
  2. Choose “Account”, then “Delete account”.
  3. Enter your password and tap “Delete for good” (an account created with Google or Apple does not ask for a password).

Your account, email, clients, rackets, strings, services, orders and photos are deleted from the database in one statement and cannot be restored. Removing the app from your phone does not delete the account. If the account is linked to Sign in with Apple and Apple issued a refresh token, we ask Apple to revoke that sign-in.

Cannot sign in? Write to support@tryrestring.com: we will make sure the account is yours (for example from a letter from the account’s email; if you hid your email at Apple, we will find another way) and delete it as soon as possible, within 30 days at the latest.

Technical copies that the hosting provider may keep for a short time (backups, logs) are overwritten in the normal course.

7. Your rights

Depending on your country (for example the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom or Ukraine) you have the right to:

  • find out whether we process your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data (most of it can be changed in the app itself);
  • delete your data (section 6);
  • restrict the processing or object to it where it is based on our legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a structured form;
  • withdraw your consent, for example by turning notifications off.

Write to support@tryrestring.com and we will reply within 30 days. So as not to hand your data to someone else, we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours (for example by sending the request from the account’s email). If you believe we have violated your rights, you can complain to the data protection authority of your country (in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights).

8. Children

re:string is a tool for racket stringers, not for children. We do not intend it for people under 16 and do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

  • The app talks to the server over HTTPS only.
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes and password reset tokens only as SHA-256 hashes. After a password change all earlier sign-ins stop working.
  • The sign-in token is kept in the iPhone’s Keychain.
  • The number of sign-in, sign-up and password reset attempts is limited.
  • The server checks every request: an account sees only its own records.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and, where required, the supervisory authorities.

10. The website tryrestring.com

The website has no cookies, analytics, advertising, third-party scripts or fonts. The website runs on Railway hosting (EU West region, Amsterdam), like the app’s server. The website’s server and the hosting provider see technical details of a request — IP address, browser type, time — and may keep logs, in order to deliver the page and protect the site. The email link opens your mail app.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. We will tell you about material changes in the app or by email.

12. Contact

Questions, requests for access or deletion, complaints: support@tryrestring.com.
