Studio for a workshop with a team

re:string already helps one stringer keep every restring under control. Studio does the same for a workshop where several people work: one client base, one stock, and you can see who does what.

Team and roles

Up to three people: owner, stringer, front desk. Each has their own login and their own rights.

Shared base and stock

Clients, rackets, prices and string stock are the same for everyone. You see when something runs low.

Queue and assignee

Every order shows who took it in and who is stringing it. The list has “Mine” and “Unassigned”.

Reports by stringer

How much each one has done and earned in a month, and the amount to pay out if you need it.

Studio is not available yet. We are building it with workshops, so it matters to us who you are and what you need. Joining the list costs nothing and commits you to nothing.
